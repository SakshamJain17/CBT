# Handwritten-register workflow

1. Create a source register and its pages.
2. Assign pages to a digitization batch.
3. A data-entry operator creates draft records, preserving page and row references and noting uncertain fields.
4. A reviewer validates the draft and reviews duplicate candidates. Candidates are never merged automatically.
5. The reviewer converts the draft into a bibliographic record and physical copy.
6. The copy remains `register_only` or `needs_review` until staff locate it physically.
7. Staff record its shelf and mark it `shelf_verified` with a verification timestamp.
8. Only a published title with a shelf-verified copy contributes to public availability.

The pilot should begin with one bounded section containing approximately 300–500 physically checked books.
